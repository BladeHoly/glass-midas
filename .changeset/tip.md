New `gm-tip`: a tip that teaches one thing, on frosted glass, with an optional icon (`slot="icon"`), a heading, actions (`.gm-tip-actions`) and a close button. Inline by default; with `anchor`, it floats next to that element (`placement` bottom or top) with an arrow, following it as the page scrolls. Closing fires a cancelable `gm-dismiss`; with `remember`, a closed tip stays closed. `dismiss()` and `show()`. In React, `GlassTip` with `onDismiss`.
