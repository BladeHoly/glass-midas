Pieces for app screens, desktop ones above all, all CSS only: `.gm-panel` (a translucent container that may hold glass), `.gm-note` (an alert that isn't glass, for inside dialogs and panels), `.gm-media-grid`/`.gm-media-tile` (thumbnails with corner labels and a status ring), `.gm-log` (an activity feed), `.gm-toggle-row` (a setting with its switch), and `.gm-kbd`, `.gm-code-inline`, `.gm-link` and `.gm-label`. `.gm-stat` takes `data-tone` on its value, with `.gm-stat-grid` and a compact density; `.gm-meter` has an indeterminate state (`aria-busy="true"`) and an optional `data-label`. `gm-dialog` takes `--gm-dialog-width`. A theme for transparent windows over the system's backdrop: `<html data-gm-theme="os-backdrop">`.
