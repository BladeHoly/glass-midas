`gm-table` filters by column: `data-filter` on a header adds a button with the column's values (with counts) to check one or several; `gm-filter` is cancelable for server-side data, with `data-filter-options` to list the values, and `filter()`, `clearFilters()` and `filters` drive it from code. React: `onFilter` on `GlassTable`.
