Form controls for inside glass: fields (`.gm-field`, `.gm-input`, `.gm-input-group`), checkboxes and radios (`.gm-check`), `gm-select` (a native select with its list on frosted glass) and `gm-otp` (a one-time code as boxes, on one real input). Callout colors now follow the status tokens, and `--gm-ink-inverse` sets what reads on a fill of the ink.
