    type: "'pill' | 'round'"

  - name: material

    description: frost blurs the backdrop heavily under a smoke tint.
  - name: radius, bezel, thickness, variant, tier
    description: Glass options. Defaults are radius 999, bezel 12, thickness 24.

`<gm-button>` puts a native `<button>` or `<a>` on glass, for actions that float over content on
their own: suggestions above an input, a toolbar over a photo. Controls inside another glass
surface are [chips](/components/chip/) instead.

## Usage

```html
<gm-button>
  <button type="button">Analyze docs</button>
</gm-button>

<gm-button shape="round">
  <a href="/new" aria-label="New"><svg>…</svg></a>
</gm-button>
```

The glass is on `gm-button`; the native control inside keeps its semantics, focus and keyboard
behavior. Give an icon-only control an `aria-label`.

## Anatomy

- **The host** is the glass pill. `shape="round"` makes it a circle.
- **The control** inside, a `button` or an `a`, fills it, and lights up on hover.

## Best practices

- **Float it over content**, not inside other glass: there, use a [chip](/components/chip/).
- **Keep labels short** and lead with an icon where one helps.
- **A few at a time.** A row of floating glass pills reads as one group; a wall of them doesn't.
