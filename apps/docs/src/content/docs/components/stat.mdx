A stat is one number: what it is, its value, how it moved against a period you name, and,
optionally, its recent shape. A meter is a share of a whole: a fill on a lighter track of the same
color. Both are content, so they're plain fills, not glass.

## Usage

<CodePanel>

```html
<article class="gm-stat">
  <p class="gm-stat-label">Revenue</p>
  <p class="gm-stat-value">$81.3K</p>
  <p class="gm-stat-delta" data-direction="up" data-tone="good">
    +23.8% <span>vs. last month</span>
  </p>
</article>

<span
  class="gm-meter"
  role="meter"
  aria-label="Disk used"
  aria-valuemin="0"
  aria-valuemax="100"
  aria-valuenow="72"
  style="--value: 0.72"
></span>
```

</CodePanel>

Both are in `css/stat.css`.

## Best practices

- **Name the period** the change is against: "vs. last month", never just "+23.8%".
- **Say whether up is good.** More revenue is good, but more refunds aren't; `data-tone` is yours
  to set.
- **Compact big numbers** ($81.3K) in a tile, and keep the exact one in a table or a tooltip.
