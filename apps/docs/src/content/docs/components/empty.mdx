An empty state is what a list says when there's nothing in it: no orders yet, nothing saved, no
results. It says why, kindly, and offers the one thing to do about it.

## Usage

<CodePanel>

```html
<div class="gm-empty">
  <svg class="gm-empty-art" aria-hidden="true">…</svg>
  <h2>No orders yet</h2>
  <p>When you buy something, it shows up here with its tracking.</p>
  <a class="gm-chip" href="/shop">Start shopping</a>
</div>
```

</CodePanel>

It's in `css/empty.css`.

## Best practices

- **Tell empty from failed.** "No orders yet" and "We couldn't load your orders" need different
  words and different actions (browse, or try again).
- **For no results, say what was searched,** and offer to clear the filters.
